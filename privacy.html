Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers Ink Horror Worker (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Ink Horror YouTube channel (@inkhorroranimated), the Ink Horror TikTok and Instagram accounts and the Ink Horror Facebook Page. The App is used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the channel's own videos to its own channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and confirm that videos go only to the Ink Horror channel.
  • Google Drive (drive): to read and write the job files and video previews the App keeps in its own "Ink Horror Pipeline" folder in the owner's Drive. Other files in the Drive are not read or changed.

When the channel owner connects Instagram and Facebook (Meta), the App requests these permissions:

  • pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management: to find the owner's own Ink Horror Facebook Page and the Instagram account linked to it.
  • pages_manage_posts: to publish the channel's own videos as Reels on that Page.
  • instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish: to show which Instagram account is connected and to publish the channel's own videos as Reels on it.

TikTok posts are made from the owner's own TikTok account in a browser window on the owner's computer; the App does not use a TikTok API.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the channel's own videos and to pass jobs between the owner's own automation tools. The App does not access any other user's account or data.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Meta

The App uses YouTube API Services and the Meta Graph API. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy, the Meta Terms of Service and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and Meta access in Facebook under Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations. Deleting the tokens from the owner's computer also ends the App's access.

Deleting your data

The App keeps no data about anyone other than the channel owner. To delete everything it stored, remove the App's access as described above and delete the tokens and logs from the owner's computer, or email the contact address below with the subject "Delete my data" and it will be removed within 30 days.
